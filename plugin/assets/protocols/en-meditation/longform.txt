Welcome. For the next few minutes there is nothing to achieve and nowhere to get to. We are going to sit with the breath, and when the mind moves away, we are going to come back. That is the whole of it.
Take a moment to settle. Feel the weight of the body on the chair or the cushion, the feet on the floor, the hands resting. Let the shoulders drop away from the ears. Let the jaw soften. Let the face be still.
Now find the breath. Not the idea of the breath, but the actual sensation of it: the cool air at the nostrils, the rise and fall of the chest, the belly moving under the hands. Choose one place, and stay there.
Breathing in, know that you are breathing in. Breathing out, know that you are breathing out. If it helps, count each out-breath, one to ten, and begin again at one. If you lose the count, that is fine. Begin again at one.
At some point you will notice that you have been thinking: planning, remembering, replaying a conversation. This is not a mistake. It is the moment the practice becomes possible. Notice it, let it be, and return to the next breath.
Now let the attention widen. Include the sounds in the room, near and far, coming and going. Include the sensations of the body as a whole. Let the breath continue in the background, and rest in this wider awareness, open, receiving whatever arrives.
Thoughts may still come. Let them pass through, like weather through an open sky. You are the sky, not the weather. Stay here for a little while, without effort.
In a moment this sit will end. Before it does, take a slightly deeper breath, and let it go slowly. Notice how you feel now compared with when you sat down. Then, when you are ready, let the eyes open, and carry a little of this stillness with you.
