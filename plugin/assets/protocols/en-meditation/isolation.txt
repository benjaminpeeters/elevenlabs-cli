Sit so that the spine is upright but not stiff, and let the hands rest wherever they are comfortable. The eyes can close, or the gaze can settle on a point on the floor a little ahead of you.
Bring the attention to the breath where it is easiest to feel: the nostrils, the chest, or the belly. There is no need to breathe in any special way. The only task is to know, each time, whether this breath is coming in or going out.
When the mind wanders, and it will, notice where it went, without any judgement, and come back to the very next breath. Every return is the practice. There is nothing else to get right.
