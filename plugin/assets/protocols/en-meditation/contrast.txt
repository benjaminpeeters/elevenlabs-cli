Partner: I have tried meditation apps a few times and I always end up just thinking about my day. Am I doing it wrong?
Candidate: No. Noticing that you were thinking about your day is the moment it works. The mind wanders; you see it; you come back. That loop is the exercise, like a repetition in the gym.
Partner: But how long before I stop drifting off like that? Weeks? Months?
Candidate: You never fully stop, and you do not need to. What changes is how quickly you notice, and how gently you return. Ten minutes a day is enough to feel that shift within a couple of weeks.
Partner: Ten minutes. And eyes closed, or open? I never know what to do with my eyes.
Candidate: Either. If you feel sleepy, keep them half open, resting on the floor ahead of you. Shall we sit for two minutes now, so you have the feeling of it before you go?
