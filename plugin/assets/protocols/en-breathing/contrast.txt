Partner: I keep hearing about this physiological sigh thing. Is it really different from just taking a deep breath?
Candidate: It is. A deep breath fills the lungs once. The double inhale reopens the small air sacs that collapse when we are stressed, and the long exhale then offloads more carbon dioxide, which is what slows the heart.
Partner: And how many of those do I do? Just one, or ten in a row before it works?
Candidate: One to three sighs is enough to take the edge off in the moment. Five minutes of them in a row, once a day, is the dose that improved mood in the study. Want to try one with me now?
Partner: Sure, let us try it. It is in through the nose and then out through the mouth, right?
Candidate: In through the nose, fully. A second sip on top. And out through the mouth, slowly, all the way. Good. Feel how the shoulders came down on their own? That is the whole trick.
