We will do four rounds of box breathing together. Each side of the box lasts four seconds: in, hold, out, hold. Follow my count, and if four feels too long today, make every side three instead.
Breathe in through the nose, slowly, for four. Two. Three. Four. Hold, gently, without straining. Two. Three. Four. Breathe out through the mouth, long and soft. Two. Three. Four. And hold, empty. Two. Three. Four.
Good. That was one round. Notice that the shoulders have dropped a little and the heart has slowed. Three more rounds now, and this time I will count only the first side; you keep the rhythm on your own.
