Welcome. This is a practice of rest, and the only thing it asks of you is to stay here, gently, for the next few minutes. If you drift, that is part of it. When you notice, come back to the sound of the voice.
Begin by feeling the whole body lying on the floor. The weight of the head. The weight of the shoulders. The back of the heart resting into the ground. The hips, heavy. The legs, long. The heels, touching the earth.
Notice the points where the body meets the floor, and let those points spread, as if the floor were rising to hold you. There is no need to hold yourself. You are held.
Now the breath. Not the breath you make, but the breath that happens by itself. Watch it come in. Watch it go out. Notice the cool air at the nostrils on the way in, the warmer air on the way out. Count, silently, from twenty-seven down to one, one number for each out-breath. If you lose the count, begin again at twenty-seven. There is no hurry.
Let the counting fade. Bring your awareness to the right side of the body. The right hand. Each finger in turn. The palm. The wrist. The forearm. The elbow. The upper arm. The shoulder. The right side of the chest. The right hip. The right thigh. The knee. The calf. The ankle. The heel. The sole of the foot. Each toe.
And now the left side, in the same order, slowly. The left hand. Each finger. The palm. The wrist. The forearm. The elbow. The upper arm. The shoulder. The left side of the chest. The hip. The thigh. The knee. The calf. The ankle. The heel. The sole. The toes.
The whole body now, all at once. Heavy. Warm. Breathing by itself. Rest here for a while.
When you are ready, let the breath deepen a little. Move the fingers. Move the toes. Take your time. There is nowhere else you need to be.
