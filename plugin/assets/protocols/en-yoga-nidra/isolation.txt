Find a comfortable position, lying on your back, with the arms a little away from the body and the palms turned upward. Let the feet fall open. There is nothing to do now, and nothing to fix.
Bring your attention to the breath, moving in and out through the nostrils. Do not change it. Simply notice its rhythm, the small pause at the top, the longer pause at the bottom.
Now let the attention travel to the right hand. The thumb. The first finger. The second finger. The third. The little finger. The palm of the hand. The back of the hand. The wrist.
