Partner: Before we start, can I ask what this practice is for? I always thought it was just falling asleep.
Candidate: Falling asleep is allowed. But the intention is different: to rest completely while a small part of you stays awake and listening.
Partner: And the counting backwards, what does that do?
Candidate: It gives the thinking mind one simple job, so the rest of you can let go. When you lose the count, that is not a failure. It is the practice working.
Partner: All right, I think I am ready. Should I close my eyes now, or wait until you say so?
Candidate: Then close the eyes, and let the floor take your weight.
