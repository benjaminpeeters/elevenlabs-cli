Partner: I am trying to learn a few greetings before my trip. How do I say hello to a group?
Candidate: 大家好。就是这三个字，"大家好"，对一群人说的时候用。
Partner: And when I meet someone I have not seen for a long time?
Candidate: 好久不见。字面上是"很久没有见到你"，朋友之间很常用，语气很轻松。
Partner: [laughs] Long time no see! That is almost the same in English.
Candidate: 对，很多人说英文的那句话就是从中文翻译过去的。你的发音很好，再说一遍试试。
