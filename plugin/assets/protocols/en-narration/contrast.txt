Partner: So this is the bay itself. I have read the numbers, but I still can't picture sixteen metres.
Candidate: Stand on the quay at low water and look at the top of the harbour wall. That is where the sea will be in six hours.
Partner: And people walk out there? On the flats?
Candidate: They did, for four thousand years, and some still do. The rule was simple: know the hour, and never trust a stranger's watch.
Partner: [laughs] That sounds like advice for more than tides.
Candidate: Most of what the tide teaches is. Shall we walk down before it turns?
